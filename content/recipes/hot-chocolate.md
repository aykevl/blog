I think this recipe was vaguely inspired by [this one](https://www.mosterdgeel.nl/recepten/chocolat-chaud/), but my version is very different.

## Ingredients

  * chocolate (I prefer dark)
  * almond or rice milk, though cow milk would probably also work
  * a little bit of chili powder
  * a little bit of [speculaaskruiden](https://verstegen.nl/products/specerijenmix-voor-koek-speculaas-glazen-strooier-34-g/) (somewhat optional, you can also try cinnamon powder)
  * Optional: some cherry liquer.

## Preparation

 1. Add a few cubes of dark chocolate (to taste) to a big mug. Add the vegan milk. Add the spices on top.
 2. Heat in a microwave until it's slightly too hot to drink.
 3. Stir well, until the chocolate has dissolved.
 4. Add a little bit of cherry liquer if you like, and stir again.
 5. Enjoy! (But try not to burn yourself).
